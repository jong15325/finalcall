1. **도메인 구조**: `com.finalcall.domain.memo.{entity,repository,service,controller,dto}`. Entity(BaseTimeEntity·soft delete·도메인 메서드)·Repository(`findByIdOrThrow`)·Service(`@Transactional(readOnly)` 기본·`@ServiceLog`·`Preconditions.validate`)·Controller(`ApiResponse<T>`).

7. spotlessApply + checkstyle 통과.
